# 3. THE PROBLEM

### Label

**Restaurant Operations Are Connected**

### Heading

**Disconnected Tools Create Disconnected Operations.**

### Body

A restaurant may have one system for orders, another for POS, another for delivery, spreadsheets for inventory, separate customer records, and manual reporting.

The problem isn't simply having multiple tools.

The problem is that the information between them often doesn't move together.

### Three Problem Cards

#### Manual Work

Staff spend time transferring information between systems, messages, spreadsheets, and devices.

#### Limited Visibility

Owners and managers may not have a complete view of what is happening across the restaurant.

#### Operational Gaps

When orders, kitchen, delivery, inventory, and customer information are disconnected, mistakes and delays become harder to identify.

### Visual

Show a **before/after comparison**.

#### Before

```text
Orders       POS       Kitchen
  ↓           ↓           ↓
WhatsApp   Spreadsheet  Phone
  ↓           ↓           ↓
Delivery   Inventory   Customers
```

Disconnected tools with broken lines.

#### After

```text
             ┌──────────────┐
             │     RMS      │
             └──────────────┘
                    │
       ┌────────────┼────────────┐
       ↓            ↓            ↓
     ORDERS        KDS          POS
       ↓            ↓            ↓
   DELIVERY     INVENTORY    CUSTOMERS
                    ↓
                ANALYTICS
```

One connected system.

# 22. FAQ

### Heading

**Frequently Asked Questions**

### 1. What is a Restaurant Management System?

A Restaurant Management System is a centralized platform used to manage important restaurant operations such as orders, POS, kitchen workflows, delivery, inventory, customers, staff, branches, and analytics.

### 2. Is RMS the same as POS?

No.

A POS primarily handles point-of-sale operations such as order entry, billing and payments.

An RMS can sit above or around these workflows and provide a broader operational view across orders, kitchen, delivery, inventory, customers, branches, staff, and business performance.

### 3. Can the RMS manage online orders?

Yes. Online orders can be brought into the central order workflow alongside orders created through POS, QR ordering, WhatsApp, phone, mobile applications, or other supported channels.

### 4. Can RMS connect with KDS?

Yes. Orders can flow from the central order management system into the kitchen display workflow.

### 5. Can it manage multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-branch functionality can provide centralized visibility while allowing individual branches to maintain their own operational information.

### 6. Can I manage restaurant staff?

Yes. Staff accounts, roles, and permissions can be configured so team members only access the functions relevant to their responsibilities.

### 7. Can RMS manage delivery riders?

Yes. Delivery management can include rider assignment, delivery statuses, zones, active deliveries, and performance. Advanced fleet functionality depends on the required setup.

### 8. Can it manage inventory?

Yes. Inventory functionality can cover stock, ingredients, recipes, suppliers, purchasing, adjustments, and wastage.

### 9. Can I see restaurant analytics?

Yes. The system can provide analytics covering areas such as revenue, orders, products, customers, branches, and operational performance.

### 10. Can I start with only the features I need?

Yes. The system can be structured around your restaurant's current requirements and expanded as your operation grows.
