It is the restaurant's **front-of-house operating interface**, connected to the kitchen, orders, tables, customers, payments, and wider restaurant management system.

### Body

During peak hours, every extra step matters.

Staff need to find products quickly, customize orders accurately, manage tables, send kitchen tickets, handle changes, and close bills without losing track of the customer or order.

# 26. FAQ

### 1. What is a restaurant POS system?

A restaurant POS system is the software used by restaurant staff to create orders, manage tables, send orders to the kitchen, process bills and payments, and complete transactions.

### 2. Is POS the same as an RMS?

No.

POS focuses primarily on front-of-house order and transaction operations.

RMS is broader and can provide centralized management across POS, orders, kitchen, delivery, inventory, customers, branches, staff, and analytics.

### 3. Can the POS handle dine-in orders?

Yes. Dine-in workflows can include table selection, open orders, additional items, bills, split payments, and checkout.

### 4. Can it handle takeaway?

Yes. Staff can create takeaway orders and move them through the kitchen and pickup workflow.

### 5. Can POS handle delivery orders?

Yes. Delivery orders can capture the required customer and delivery information and move into the restaurant's delivery workflow.

### 6. Can POS send orders to the kitchen?

Yes. Orders can be sent into a connected KDS or kitchen workflow.

### 7. Can customers customize products?

Yes. Products can support sizes, modifiers, add-ons, removals, and special instructions depending on the restaurant configuration.

### 8. Can POS manage restaurant tables?

Yes. Dine-in POS can be connected with table management, including table status, open orders, guests, and active checks.

### 9. Can I split a restaurant bill?

Yes. Split-bill workflows can divide bills by item, guest, equal portions, or available payment methods depending on configuration.

### 10. Can different staff have different permissions?

Yes. Roles and permissions can control access to functions such as discounts, refunds, order editing, and reports.

### 11. Can POS work with multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-branch POS can be configured with branch-specific information and centralized management where required.

### 12. Can POS connect with my existing systems?

Potentially, depending on the system and available integration/API. We can assess your existing POS, payment, kitchen, ordering, and other systems before implementation.
