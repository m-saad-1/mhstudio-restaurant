# Online Ordering Systems

## Page Goal

Position MhStudio's Online Ordering System as a **direct digital ordering channel owned by the restaurant**.

The core story is:

**Discover Menu → Customize → Cart → Checkout → Order → Kitchen → Fulfillment → Customer**

The page should make clear that online ordering is more than putting a menu on a website. It is a complete ordering experience that can connect with the restaurant's RMS, POS, KDS, delivery, customer management, and analytics.

# 3. THE PROBLEM

### Label

**Third-Party Dependency**

### Heading

**Your Customers Shouldn't Have to Leave Your Brand to Place an Order.**

### Body

Third-party marketplaces can help restaurants reach customers, but restaurants can also benefit from having their own direct digital ordering channel.

A branded ordering system gives the restaurant more control over the customer experience and creates a direct connection between the customer and the restaurant.

### Problem Cards

#### Another Customer Journey

Customers interact with a marketplace instead of your own brand.

#### Limited Brand Experience

Your menu and ordering experience live inside someone else's platform.

#### Fragmented Data

Customer and order information may not live inside your own restaurant ecosystem.

#### Less Direct Engagement

It can be harder to build a long-term direct relationship with customers.

# 30. FAQ

### 1. What is an online ordering system for restaurants?

It is a digital ordering platform that allows customers to browse a restaurant's menu, customize products, choose fulfillment options, complete checkout, and place orders directly with the restaurant.

### 2. Can customers order directly from my website?

Yes. Your restaurant website can include a complete direct ordering experience.

### 3. Can customers order through a mobile application?

Yes. A branded restaurant mobile application can provide the same core ordering experience on mobile.

### 4. Can customers choose delivery or pickup?

Yes. The ordering flow can support delivery, pickup, or other configured fulfillment options.

### 5. Can I accept cash on delivery?

Yes, where your restaurant's delivery workflow supports it.

### 6. Can customers customize food items?

Yes. Products can support sizes, modifiers, add-ons, removals, and special instructions based on your menu configuration.

### 7. Can online orders go directly to the kitchen?

Yes. Where the required POS/RMS/KDS workflow is configured, online orders can move into the kitchen preparation workflow.

### 8. Can I manage delivery areas?

Yes. Delivery zones, fees, minimums, and branch coverage can be configured according to your restaurant's delivery model.

### 9. Can customers track their orders?

Order status tracking can be provided throughout the configured fulfillment workflow. Real-time map tracking depends on the delivery setup and available integrations.

### 10. Can customers create accounts?

Yes. Customer accounts can support profiles, saved information, order history, favorites, and reordering where configured.

### 11. Can I run online ordering for multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-branch ordering can be configured with branch-specific menus, locations, hours, availability, and fulfillment rules.

### 12. Can online ordering connect to my existing POS?

Potentially. This depends on the POS and its available integration/API. We can assess the existing system before implementation.

### 13. Do I need a mobile app to offer online ordering?

No.

A responsive website can provide a complete online ordering experience. A mobile application can be added later for restaurants that want a dedicated channel for repeat customers, reordering, offers, and notifications.

### 14. Can customers order through WhatsApp?

Yes, depending on the implementation. A restaurant can use WhatsApp as an ordering channel, with the order then being handled according to the configured workflow.

# 35. CORE POSITIONING

Do not position online ordering as:

**"An online menu with an order button."**

Position it as:

**"A complete direct ordering channel that connects your customers with your restaurant's operations."**

The distinction between the four core products should remain clear:

```text id="p6x8cq"
WEBSITE / APP
Customer-facing digital experience

        ↓

ONLINE ORDERING
Customer ordering & checkout

        ↓

RMS
Central restaurant management

        ↓

POS
In-store order & transaction operations

        ↓

KDS
Kitchen preparation

        ↓

DELIVERY
Order fulfillment
```

This hierarchy should be consistent across all MhStudio service pages.
