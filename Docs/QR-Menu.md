# 5. Table-Specific QR Codes

### Heading

**Every Table Can Have Its Own Ordering Identity.**

Each table can have a unique QR code connected to its table number.

When a guest scans the QR code:

* The restaurant is identified
* The branch is identified
* The table is identified
* The customer does not need to manually enter the table number

### Example

`Table QR #T-14`

Customer scans.

System automatically identifies:

**Branch:** Main Branch
**Table:** T-14

The order then enters the restaurant's central ordering system with the correct table attached.

### Important UX Rule

Do not ask customers to type their table number unless there is a specific fallback scenario.

The QR code should handle table identification automatically.

# 15. Customer Payment

### Heading

**Make Checkout More Flexible.**

Depending on the restaurant's setup, QR ordering can support different payment flows.

### Option 1 — Pay at Counter

Customer places order and pays at the counter.

### Option 2 — Pay Through Staff

Staff handles payment through the POS.

### Option 3 — Online Payment

Customer completes payment through an available online payment method.

### Option 4 — Pay After Dining

The restaurant maintains the table order and closes the bill through the normal POS workflow.

The available payment methods should be configurable based on the restaurant's operation and payment integrations.

# 29. FAQ

### Do customers need to install an app?

No. The QR menu can be accessed directly from a smartphone browser.

### Does every table get a different QR code?

Yes, table-specific QR codes can identify the branch and table automatically.

### Can customers customize their food?

Yes. Products can support sizes, modifiers, add-ons, and special instructions.

### Can QR orders go directly to the kitchen?

Yes, when connected with the restaurant's KDS/order workflow.

### Can QR ordering connect to the POS?

Yes. QR orders can be integrated into the restaurant's central order and POS workflow.

### Can customers place multiple orders?

Yes. Additional orders can be associated with the same table/dining session where configured.

### Can we still use waiters?

Absolutely. QR ordering is designed to work alongside restaurant staff, not necessarily replace them.

### Can we use it across multiple branches?

Yes. Branch-specific menus, tables, prices, availability, and QR configurations can be supported.

### Can we use the same menu on our website?

Yes. The digital menu infrastructure can be connected to other ordering channels.

### Can we add promotions?

Yes. Deals, add-ons, combos, recommendations, and promotional content can be configured.

### Can customers pay online?

Online payment can be supported where the required payment gateway/integration is available.
