# Kitchen Display Systems (KDS)

## Page Goal

Position MhStudio KDS as the **digital command center for restaurant kitchens**.

The page should make it immediately clear that KDS replaces scattered paper tickets and verbal communication with a structured, real-time kitchen workflow.

Core story:

**Order Received → Kitchen Queue → Preparation → Ready → Fulfillment**

The KDS should be presented as a system that connects the **POS, online ordering, RMS, kitchen staff, preparation stations, and fulfillment workflows**.

# 27. FAQ

### 1. What is a Kitchen Display System?

A KDS is a digital kitchen workflow system that displays restaurant orders to kitchen staff and helps teams organize preparation and completion.

### 2. Is KDS the same as a kitchen printer?

No.

A kitchen printer produces physical tickets.

A KDS provides a digital workflow where staff can view, organize, update, and track orders electronically.

### 3. Can KDS receive orders from POS?

Yes. Orders created through POS can be sent into the KDS workflow.

### 4. Can online orders appear on KDS?

Yes. Online ordering channels can feed into the central restaurant order workflow and then into KDS where the required integration is configured.

### 5. Can KDS handle dine-in orders?

Yes. Dine-in tickets can display the relevant table and order information.

### 6. Can KDS handle delivery orders?

Yes. Delivery orders can be displayed with the relevant customer/order information and moved to Ready before entering the delivery workflow.

### 7. Can different kitchen stations have different screens?

Yes. For restaurants with multiple preparation areas, KDS can be structured around stations and routing requirements.

### 8. Can kitchen staff see modifiers?

Yes. Product modifiers and special instructions can be displayed directly on the relevant ticket.

### 9. Can KDS track preparation time?

Yes. Order timestamps and status transitions can be used to calculate preparation and operational timing metrics.

### 10. Can managers see kitchen performance?

Yes. Where analytics are configured, managers can review preparation times, order volume, delays, station workload, and other operational metrics.

### 11. Can KDS work with my existing POS?

Potentially. This depends on the POS and the available integration/API. We can assess the existing setup before implementation.

### 12. Does KDS require a separate tablet or display?

The deployment depends on the restaurant's setup. KDS can be designed for suitable touch displays, tablets, terminals, or dedicated kitchen screens.

**"A real-time digital workflow for organizing and managing restaurant kitchen preparation."**

The most important relationship is:

```text id="3px0v6"

      ↓

      ↓
CENTRAL ORDER MANAGEMENT

      ↓
    READY

TABLE / PICKUP / DELIVERY
```
