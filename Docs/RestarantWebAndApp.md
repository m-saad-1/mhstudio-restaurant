# 16. FAQ

### Heading

**Frequently Asked Questions**

### 1. Do you build both restaurant websites and mobile applications?

Yes. We can build a responsive restaurant website, a branded mobile application, or both as part of one connected digital ecosystem.

### 2. Can customers order directly from the website?

Yes. The website can include a direct online ordering experience for available pickup, delivery, or dine-in workflows.

### 3. Can the website and mobile app use the same menu?

Yes. They can be designed to work from the same restaurant ecosystem so the digital experience remains consistent.

### 4. Can the website connect with a POS or restaurant management system?

Yes. Where the required integration/API is available, the website and ordering system can connect with POS, RMS, KDS, delivery, payment, or other restaurant systems.

### 5. Can I start with only a website?

Yes. A restaurant can start with a website and later expand into a branded mobile application and additional restaurant systems.

### 6. Can customers make reservations from the website?

Yes. Reservation functionality can be incorporated based on the restaurant's reservation workflow and requirements.

### 7. Can I have multiple restaurant branches?

Yes. Multi-location experiences can be designed for restaurant groups, including branch-specific information and ordering workflows.

### 8. Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. All restaurant websites are designed responsively for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.

### 9. Can my restaurant app support online ordering?

Yes. A branded application can support menu browsing, cart and checkout, ordering, customer accounts, order history, and other required customer experiences.

### 10. Do I need an app if I already have a website?

Not necessarily.

A website is usually the right starting point for most restaurants. A mobile application becomes more valuable when you have a strong base of repeat customers and want a dedicated channel for ordering, reordering, offers, notifications, and customer engagement.
